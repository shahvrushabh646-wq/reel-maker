# Instagram Edits music workflow — @festivalofbharat

The reel-maker is used for the visual production. Final Instagram music should be added in Instagram Edits.

1. Build the 9:16 reel in this app.
2. Use **Edits music mode** conceptually: do not rely on the app's catalog music for the final Instagram post.
3. Export the visual master.
4. Open Instagram Edits and import the visual reel.
5. Add the final song/audio using Instagram/Edits' own music and audio tools.
6. Finish captions, cover and publishing settings for @festivalofbharat.

The app must not scrape or download Instagram's licensed music library. The existing universal music search remains available for legal/user-provided audio previews.

Important current limitation: the browser export is WebM, not guaranteed MP4/H.264. A separate MP4/H.264 export step is required for a fully Edits-ready file.
